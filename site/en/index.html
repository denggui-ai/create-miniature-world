THE CONNECTION MATTERS

Why does the story
happen here?

The mug was used,
so the track is there.

The figures don't have to sit on the mug. A coffee ring is the trace of a mug in use, and a ring is exactly the shape of a track: children race around it, grandma cheers from outside, and the mug stands beside them just as it is.

When we judge an idea, we ask: swap out the object, and does the most interesting connection in the picture still hold?

THE CREATIVE PROCESS

Clever ideas,
step by step.

From reading the object
to reading the picture, in four steps.

01

Find the link

Spot the feature of the object
worth seeing in a new way.

02

Build the scene

Let an action or a space
bring that link to life.

03

Compose the picture

Keep the object, the new characters
and the overall look working together.

04

Check the idea

Look at what is visible, and tell apart
a working idea, a good look and a faithful product.

PLAN THE SHOT

Picture it first,
then make it.

Where the camera is, who is doing what,
and which part of the object becomes what.

A

Generate the whole picture

When you only give the object's name, or your photo isn't right to build on, the whole image is generated fresh.

B

Add figures to your photo

When you have a photo taken close to the table, with the object large and a clean background, it keeps your photo and only adds figures and props.

Afterwards it asks you just one thing: "What is this scene?" If you read the story at a glance, it worked.

WHAT THE SKILL DOES

From one sentence
to one picture.

The Skill handles the idea;
your Codex or Claude Code makes the image.

01

It picks the ideas

Start with an object's name or a product photo. By default you get two different ideas, one image each; if you have an idea, it follows yours. Want to see the plans first? Just say so.

02

How small is small

Figures are sized against parts of the object, like "a person about as tall as one hole", so the scene reads as a tiny world.

03

Reference and edits

Your product photo sets how the object looks; built-in photos of real model figures make the people look like actual painted miniatures. Name the image you want changed, and it edits that one once.

04

Original and finish

The original is always kept. Ask for film grain or a light depth-of-field effect and it saves a separate version.

What each version actually tested is in the release notes (Chinese).

WITH APPRECIATION

A tribute to a way
of seeing the world.

Inspired by Tatsuya Tanaka (田中達也) and his mitate (見立て) approach, we are learning to find another world inside ordinary things, and to keep exploring with our own objects and scenes.

Miniature World is an independent creative tool for AI miniature photography and tiny-scene making. It has no official partnership with or endorsement from Tatsuya Tanaka, MINIATURE CALENDAR, Preiser or any product brand shown in the examples.
